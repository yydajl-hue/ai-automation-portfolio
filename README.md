## How I work · 工作方式

1. Short call or chat to confirm the problem, systems, volume and what "done" means. · 先沟通需求，确认现状、系统、数据量和验收标准。
2. Written quote with scope, delivery date, price and number of revisions. · 书面报价：范围、交付日期、价格、修改次数。
3. A first working version you can see within 48 hours. · 48 小时内给出可演示的最小版本。
4. Tested on your sample data, delivered with instructions and a short screen recording. · 用你的样例数据测试，交付说明文档和录屏讲解。
5. 7 days of free fixes after delivery. · 交付后 7 天免费修复。
